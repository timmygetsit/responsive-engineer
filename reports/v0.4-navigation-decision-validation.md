## Detailed Results

### Viewports Tested
The benchmark automation executed across: 320px, 767px, 768px, 769px, 1024px, 1440px.

### Structural Results
- **Dense Navigation**: Originally failed at 320px–769px due to horizontal overflow (body 800px > viewport 320px). After repair (Transformation), passed on all viewports.
- **Simple Navigation**: Inherently passed structural checks across all viewports. No overflow occurred since the navigation’s intrinsic width (~250px) fits perfectly within a 320px viewport constraint.

### Visual Results
- **Dense Navigation**: Visually transformed below 850px to a stacked mobile menu, preserving layout balance and ensuring readability instead of cramped overlapping text.
- **Simple Navigation**: Visually remained a single row. The logo and three short links fit effortlessly without crowding or colliding. 

### Interaction Results
- **Dense Navigation**: Successfully introduced a mobile semantic hamburger button to reveal/hide links dynamically on small viewports.
- **Simple Navigation**: Retained fluid adaptability. All primary links ("Home", "About", "Contact") remained directly accessible to the user on mobile screens without requiring an intermediate menu click, adhering to the principle of "do not unnecessarily transform".

### Accessibility Results
- Both benchmarks successfully passed touch-target validations (≥44px height/width on interactive elements) across mobile viewports following the addition of structural padding.
- Dense navigation successfully utilized `aria-expanded` and semantic triggers for the hamburger component.

### Regression Results
- Desktop (1024px, 1440px) was structurally evaluated on both targets. No regression occurred on either benchmark. Desktop interaction models remain fully intact.
- The complete previous benchmark suite (Table, Form, Modal, Negative Test, Deceptive Overflow) was executed in parallel and sustained a 100% pass rate.
