## v0.4 Scope
- Established the fundamental distinction between **Fluid Adaptation** and **Interaction Transformation**.
- Validated that dense desktop layouts can be properly transformed into mobile-friendly structural equivalents (e.g., Hamburger Menus) below appropriate breakpoints.
- Validated a negative false-positive case ensuring that simple navigations gracefully scale down fluidly *without* introducing unnecessary interaction bottlenecks.
- Established strict mobile-ux accessibility checks ensuring `aria` attributes and `min-height`/`min-width` touch targets (44px) are preserved.

## Known Limitations
- Breakpoint determinations (e.g. at what exact width does "too dense" begin?) still partially rely on agentic visual heuristic judgment rather than strict mathematical thresholds.
- Purely stylistic/visual aesthetics are extremely difficult to automate without snapshot baselines, meaning the agent might theoretically create a structurally sound but "ugly" adaptation.
